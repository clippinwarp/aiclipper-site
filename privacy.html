Privacy Policy

AIClipper · Effective 23 September 2026

AIClipper is a single-user tool: it is operated exclusively by its developer, on the developer's own computer, for the developer's own TikTok account. It has no other users, and this policy is short because there is very little to disclose.

What data the tool handles

  • OAuth tokens for the operator's own TikTok account, granted by the operator through TikTok's standard consent flow. Tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer and are never transmitted to anyone other than TikTok's API.
  • The operator's own content and its metadata: video files, captions, scheduled posting times, and (via TikTok's API) view/engagement statistics for the operator's own posts.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or process data about any TikTok user other than its operator.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer data to third parties.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics trackers, or cookies — including on this website, which is a static page that sets no cookies.

Data retention and deletion

All data lives on the operator's own machine and can be deleted at any time by the operator. TikTok access can be revoked at any moment from the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Connected apps, which immediately invalidates the tool's authorization.
